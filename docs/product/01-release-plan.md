## Product brief

Levelmaxing helps a person plan training, record what they actually do and eat, and use that evidence to decide what to change next. The core interaction is a loop. Open Today, do a workout or log a meal, see it reflected in Progress, then review a suggested adjustment when there is enough evidence. Logging must work inside a gym or while travelling with unreliable connectivity. A second device must recover the same confirmed history without making the user re-enter it.

**Confirmed.** The product name is Levelmaxing. The request calls for a working prototype and a plan for the full apps with integrated backend, cross-device sync, low operating cost, speed and non-breaking updates. The attached research supplies the functional scope. The output workspace contains the prototype and planning artifacts. Production implementation starts in the owner's chosen repository and environment.

**Proposed.** Launch for adults in India with English and Hindi interfaces, regional food coverage, editable portions, and kg/lb and grams/household units. Preserve the ability to launch elsewhere through a region-capability catalog. Core manual logging, history, export, and deletion stay available without a subscription. Premium can cover expanded content, managed coaching, and metered AI. Confirm pricing, entitlements, places covered, and exact support policy before configuring stores.

**Reference scope.** The attachment uses the public Built With Science+ product as a feature reference. Public material supports training, nutrition, coaching, progress, and a large education catalog. Exact proprietary calculations, internal entitlement rules, and every private screen are unknown. The published workout check-in timing varies by source. Levelmaxing will use its own reviewed eligibility and original content, layout and voice. App-store notes also identify optional regional and platform-specific features, which should be labeled by actual availability. [Public product and release notes](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/built-with-science/id6446000532) · [Public product updates](https://builtwithscience.com/whats-new-2/)

**Product boundaries.** Consumer training and wellness for adults. Diagnosis, treatment, fertility or contraception claims, precise body-composition inference from photos or scale data, automatic purchase or provider sharing, a social network, a coaching marketplace, and a standalone Wear OS app fall outside this release. Samsung interoperability uses supported Health Connect data on the phone. Apple Watch is a paired companion with disconnected capture and automatic recovery for a bound session.

### Users and journeys

| User | Primary journey | Completion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New gym or home trainee | Onboard → choose schedule and equipment → review plan → log first session | A feasible plan and completed session appear in history on both phones. |
| Returning lifter | Today → resume current session → see prior loads → log sets → finish → review recap | Rest, substitutions, and corrections survive interruptions. Recap derives from saved sets. |
| Person tracking food | Today → add food by search, scan, text or quick entry → review portions → save | Diary totals change once based on the confirmed record and show provenance. |
| Person adjusting a goal | Progress → open check-in → inspect evidence → accept, decline or revisit | Accepted changes take effect on the displayed date. Prior history is preserved. |
| Watch or health-platform user | Pair or authorize → log or sync → review source and freshness | Duplicate reads and reconnection leave one record. Revocation leaves manual logging available. |
| Content editor and support operator | Draft → review rights and accuracy → publish/revoke or resolve a failed job | Changes are auditable. Support has scoped access and can guide recovery without disclosing private records. |

### Quality and success

- **Durability.** Every visibly saved offline log survives process death. Durable local changes and pending operations commit together. After reconnect, the server preserves one logical action and converges without silent overwrites. Account switches, restores and stale devices are explicit test cases.
- **Fast paths.** Local actions render without requiring a round trip. Workout rest and logging continue during provider failure. Initial proposed targets are p95 at most 100 ms for a durable local save and 400 ms for regional server push/pull/search processing, with typical foreground convergence within 5 seconds under healthy networking. Proposed core service objective is 99.9% with a daily-backup recovery target of at most 24 hours of loss and 8 hours to restore service. Commit targets after device baseline, measured workload, and recovery drill. [Production architecture and measurement scope](02-production-architecture.md)
- **Compatibility.** Old supported released builds can still sign in, open persisted state, write and finish a workout, log a meal, sync their pending queue, and export data throughout schema expansion and backend rollback. Introduce capabilities additively and test archived clients.
- **Privacy and accessibility.** Validate auth and consent at action boundaries, minimize logged/third-party data, use screen reader labels and non-color status. Verify large accessibility text, theme modes, captioning, keyboard routes in admin, and layout at phone and tablet widths. Determine actual supported OS versions with vendor, native SDK and hardware evidence.
- **Suggested product measurements.** Review first completed session, first confirmed food entry, successful on-device save, successful sync after reconnect, median correction time for a scan, confirmed workout edits, and an accepted/rejected check-in funnel. Baseline these during consenting beta cohorts. Backend telemetry should count events and latency without recording meal wording, cycle events, image URLs or unneeded health values.

| Coaching, progress and native surfaces | Reviewed adapting flows, offline recovery, paired Watch, HealthKit/Health Connect and external export | Weeks 9–19 |
| Operations, privacy and localization | Commercial and rights workflows, grocery, catalog coverage, accessible localization, recovery instrumentation | Weeks 15–25 |
| Full-release verification | Store sandboxes, QA hardware matrix, content audit, regression, load/restore and controlled rollout | Weeks 23–30 |

**Staffing assumption.** Two engineers covering shared mobile and backend, one engineer covering native integrations and release with overlapping feature work, plus part-time product design, QA and specialist review. An editorial owner, video/translation capacity, privacy counsel and account/store owners are separately required. A solo build with external specialists may reasonably take 8–14+ calendar months. Re-estimate after S02 and initial coverage/market/provider validation. Cost gates and measured load thresholds are in the [architecture](02-production-architecture.md).

## Verification and rollout

| Gate | Evidence to attach to the requirement ledger |
| --- | --- |
| **V01 · Cross-device integrity** | Save offline, kill and reopen, reconcile two clients. Interleave different children and identical record edits. Replay same request after response loss and reject key reuse with changed body. Delete on A and edit on B, resolve explicitly, confirm no resurrection. Verify late old-account responses and lost credentials cannot expose another cache. |
| **V02 · Historical math and versions** | Fixtures for preparation units, yield, edible portions, actual vs prescribed sets, duration, check-in evidence, missing nutrients, and DST. Download a pre-edit historical report, edit a recipe/catalog/plan, verify prior result is preserved. |
| **V03 · Provider and media failure** | Network timeout, malformed response, duplicate webhook, lease expiry, low budget and cancellation with late provider output. Real client observes retry/status and can still log manually. Reject cross-account access to private media and export links through a real authorized test endpoint. |
| **V04 · Native devices** | Supported low-end and current iOS/Android, at least one compatible Samsung phone, paired physical Watch, and platform with unsupported/denied capability. Test background/foreground, reconnect, rest timer, dynamic targets, physical camera, HealthKit/Health Connect permissions, ActivityKit lock-screen state, and supported Strava account. |
| **V05 · Older releases and recovery** | Start with authentic earlier app state including encrypted persisted pending changes. Launch through real sign-in/restore, execute, reopen, and exercise older API contracts. Run staged expand/contract and rollback with a human-prepared isolated database. Simulate lease expiry, stale cursors and restored dataset generation without allowing an old worker to accept writes. |
| **V06 · Ease, accessibility and localization** | With synthetic assigned tasks, complete onboarding, offline set, food logging, progress review and data settings. Inspect keyboard/screen-reader, dark/light, accessibility text, right-to-left readiness, labels, translations, units and private notifications. Require captions/transcripts on published assets. |
| **V07 · Quality and operating economics** | Evaluate held-out weighed Indian meals and package labels by locale/family: food recognition, portion/nutrient error distribution, correction effort, latency, rejection rate and cost. Report sample size and low-confidence cohorts. Run scoped load and reconnect bursts, record size/throughput and compare with proposed latency and budget. |
| **V08 · Billing, privacy and operator drills** | Sandbox outcomes verify account-bound entitlements, duplicate/out-of-order changes, purchases and restore without cross-account leakage. Inspect secure export and owner-witnessed deletion/restore workflow. Audit role permissions, redaction, rights and alert ownership. |
| **V09 · Public release** | Signed store candidates and screenshots from final builds, capability matrix, original content/rights manifest, accepted privacy/legal copy and store declarations, pricing review, support runbooks and rollback rehearsal. Expand controlled cohorts only after health review with the operator on call. |

For live endpoint checks, record actual routes, safe target, sanitized response shape and expected post-state. For each group report verified evidence, failed evidence or the exact unverified dependency. Use approved synthetic fixtures and isolated resources. Migration and schema application, resets, destructive database operations, production credentials/configuration, store publishing and rollout are human-owned. Commands must be inspected for implicit setup or cleanup before use.

**Prototype boundary.** The clickable local prototype is an evidence surface for selected journeys and sync behavior. Its own launch and test record identifies exactly what ran. Native binaries, managed backend, provider credentials, commercial stores, full licensed content and production operations need the release evidence above.
